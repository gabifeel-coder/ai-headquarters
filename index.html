Publishing & visitor privacy

PUBLIC-SITE READY

This is a static website. Once hosted on an HTTPS address, anyone with the URL can open it without an account. Your projects, tasks and business records stay inside your browser. Each visitor starts with a separate, empty workspace. No shared accounts, cloud sync, analytics or AI model are connected.
